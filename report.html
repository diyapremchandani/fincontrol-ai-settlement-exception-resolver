Chargebacks could not be resolved: 0 of 2 correct.

In this dataset a chargeback reduces the bank credit with no supporting record anywhere, which is exactly what an unexplained shortfall looks like. The two are indistinguishable from settlement and bank data alone, so the resolver returned "unresolvable" and referred both rather than guessing. That is the intended behaviour, and it is why neither wrong answer reached the ledger.

Closing this needs a fourth source: the disputes feed. It was found by measuring the system, not by anticipating it.

Accuracy is measured against a held-out answer key the resolver never saw. The dataset is deliberately exception-heavy at 20% so every reason code is exercised; a real merchant runs nearer 2–5%. Amounts in rupees.
